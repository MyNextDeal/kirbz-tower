The Vision

A show piece that stands out in the city.

Kirbz Tower is the real building — the thing my whole life has been leading to. Hotel, condos, residences. Green walls and community floors. A free recording studio for artists. A free language school. An AI operating system running the entire tower. Built to last centuries.

Developing my world.
The Origin

I've been building my entire life.

Legos on the floor. Tree forts in the branches. Climbing as high as the tree would let me. I've always loved skyscrapers and architecture — and I've always been fascinated with the Twin Towers. I wanted to be an architect.

Life gave me a bigger title: developer. In the physical world — real estate. In the virtual world — software. Same word. Same person. Both worlds. Kirbz Tower is where those two lives stop being two.

The Building

Built to last centuries. Not decades.

LEED healthy-energy specifications

Kind to the people inside it and the world around it.

Living green everywhere

Green walls, gardens, and trees in the sky.

Every kind of home

Affordable housing, condos, penthouses, a hotel — a vertical neighborhood where everyone belongs.

A free recording studio

Where artists make real songs and collaborate. Music lives inside the building.

A free language school

English, Spanish, Chinese — whatever people want, with incentives and prizes for fluency. Suelta Academy, given a campus.

An AI operating system for the tower

The whole building runs on it — current, alive, futuristic.

Top-of-the-line amenities

Sauna, cold plunge, coffee lounges — spaces built for people to stay a while.

Mixed-use at the base

Restaurants and street life at the bottom of the tower.

A helipad at the top

AI robots helping around

Community floors

Floors designed for events, giving back, and bringing people together.

A legacy in stone and glass

The Life Inside

Positivity as a design principle — a place that brings people together and makes them feel part of something.

Not a building people pass through. A building people belong to.
